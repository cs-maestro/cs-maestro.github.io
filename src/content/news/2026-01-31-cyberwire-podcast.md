I joined Dave Bittner on the [N2K CyberWire Research Saturday podcast](https://thecyberwire.com/podcasts/research-saturday/410/notes) to discuss our [research on SMS-delivered URLs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09232). The work also received coverage from [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/01/millions-of-people-imperiled-through-sign-in-links-sent-by-sms/), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/millions-of-smartphone-users-could-be-at-risk-from-the-sms-sign-in-url-issue-heres-what-you-need-to-look-out-for), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/01/23/sms-private-urls-data-exposure-study/), and [CERT Azerbaijan](https://cert.gov.az/en/news/sms-delivered-login-links-have-created-security-risks-for-millions-of-users-9097).
